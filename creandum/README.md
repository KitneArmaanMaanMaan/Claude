## The three findings the pack turns on

1. **The Euro Seed 50 is Creandum's funnel, not their publicity.** They converted 8 of the 50
   into investments. Pitching a name from that list is disqualifying; the target is a company
   that would make next year's list and isn't on this one.
2. **Cybersecurity, defence and edge AI each have zero portfolio companies** across 154
   records, despite Creandum publicly arguing for all three. Climate and energy has been cold
   since October 2024. That is where a pick should land.
3. **Squid has never priced a round.** UK register, read 11 Sep 2026: share capital £1 since
   incorporation, no SH01 ever filed, both founders still PSCs at 25–50%. The money in is on
   unconverted SAFEs. A lead investor would be setting the first price — while the company is
   already in production at National Grid Electricity Distribution.
