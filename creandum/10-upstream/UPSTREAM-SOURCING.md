# Sourcing upstream of the funding event — what it actually produces

The first longlist was 153 companies, 153 of which had already been validated by YC or a press
release. This is what the same brief produces when those two channels are banned outright.

**Channels used:** EIC Accelerator beneficiary PDFs (Jan–Mar 2026 and Oct 2025 cut-offs, pulled
direct from eic.ec.europa.eu) · Oxford University Innovation companies-formed register ·
Chalmers Ventures portfolio register · Conception X · Station F Future 40 · Concept VC and
Seedcamp portfolios · Hacker News Algolia API, date-windowed · GitHub ·
**Apple App Store top-free RSS feeds, country by country, for 26 European markets.**

**The channel finding that matters most:** the App Store country charts were the highest-yield
original source, and nobody reads the Latvian or Romanian charts. A Finnish app charting in
Denmark is a signal no database carries. This was pulled as a single snapshot of the top 40–50;
the right version takes the top 200, adds Google Play, and diffs week-over-week so you see
*risers* rather than incumbents. That is where a company appears three months before anyone
writes about it — and it maps directly onto what Creandum actually buys.

## Best fit to the pattern

### Bought — Helsinki · bought.app
Secondhand marketplace that auto-generates listings from your email receipts and photos, then
delivers via Wolt in under an hour.
**Closed market:** "Vinted won resale." The counter-claim is that the real gate is listing
friction — about ten minutes an item — and that collapsing it to near-zero converts the ~90%
of wardrobes nobody ever lists.
**Stage:** $1.5M seed, Lifeline Ventures; angels include Wolt's COO, Swappie's founder and an
early Bolt investor. Has rolled up Zadaa and Netflea (+500k users).
**Channel:** charting #28 in *Denmark* — a Finnish app in a foreign Nordic market.
*Closest thing in the set to Creandum's own Depop, in their home region.*

### Clearcam — Dublin · clearcam.org
On-device AI object detection, tracking and search added to any existing RTSP/IP CCTV camera.
**Closed market:** AI video analytics was a €500–1,500/camera enterprise VMS product. Running
YOLOv8+ByteTrack locally on hardware the business already owns takes per-camera cost down
roughly **two orders of magnitude** and removes the cloud-upload consent problem entirely.
**Stage: no funding round found at all.** Solo founder, iOS and Android live, premium
subscription. **The purest "no round" candidate in the entire project.**
**Channel:** Show HN (233 points) plus GitHub traction.

### MakeSense Technology — London · makesense.technology
"Shape" — a torch-shaped haptic device that bends to point a blind or partially sighted user
in the right direction.
**Closed market:** independent navigation has meant a guide dog — roughly **£38,000 lifetime
cost and a multi-year waiting list** — or a human guide. A handheld device at consumer
electronics price is a genuine order-of-magnitude access collapse.
**Evidence:** *Nature Scientific Reports* paper (Dec 2024) showing users matching sighted
performance on a location task.
**Stage:** ~$3.5M total — £2.5M seed (Eka Ventures), £500k Innovate UK grant. No Series A.
**Channel:** EIC Accelerator Jan–Mar 2026 beneficiary list.

### Soihtu DTx (Meliora) — Espoo · soihtudtx.com
A prescription video game for major depressive disorder.
**Closed market:** depression treatment is rationed by therapist supply — months of Nordic
waiting lists. A prescribable game collapses cost per treated patient from ~€1,500 of
clinician time toward software marginal cost.
**Evidence:** published RCT, plus a March 2026 medRxiv paper showing no increase in
gaming-related problems. Unusually strong for the stage.
**Stage:** ~€3M pre-seed, Innovestor and NewFund HEKA. No Series A.

### Oxailis — Oxford, spun out Dec 2025
Measures tissue perfusion from **standard existing ultrasound hardware, with no contrast
agent**; first product is first-trimester placental assessment.
**Closed market:** perfusion imaging has been locked behind contrast MRI/CT and specialist
reading. Doing it on the probe already in every antenatal clinic collapses cost per assessment
by ~10–100x and moves pre-eclampsia detection to the routine 12-week scan.
**Evidence:** 17 years of the Oxford Placenta Imaging group, validated across 7,500+ pregnancies.
**Stage: no disclosed funding round.** *Flag: no standalone website found; verify with OUI.*

### Safer Birth — Oxford, spun out March 2026
Flags babies at higher risk of intrapartum harm at the onset of labour, from a database of
**71,000+ births**, giving 8+ hours of warning.
**Closed market:** continuous intrapartum risk assessment has required CTG plus expert
obstetric judgment, available only where that judgment is. **Stage: no disclosed round.**

### Radiant Science — London · radiantscience.io
Voice agents answering patient calls and managing bookings for clinics.
**Closed market:** a single-site private clinic cannot afford a full-time medical secretary
(~£30k) and simply loses the calls it can't answer. A few hundred pounds a month makes
reception economics work at one-practitioner scale — the iZettle move applied to clinic admin.
**Stage:** pre-seed, Calm/Storm. Already deployed with recurring revenue. Conception X CX24.

### Nano Dentica — Gothenburg · nanodentica.com
Industrial-scale bioactive fluorapatite — the mineral of tooth enamel — for restorative
dental materials.
**Closed market:** secondary caries under fillings is treated as inevitable; every filling is
assumed to fail and be redone. A material that rebuilds enamel attacks a multi-billion-euro
re-treatment cycle the profession had priced in as permanent.
**Stage:** pre-seed, Chalmers Ventures, raising with Almi Invest.

## Honest limits, stated by the sourcing agent

- **Funding stage is the weakest column.** For Qantum, Billabex, Vyse Tech, Skyfora, MUMILK and
  YowPay no disclosed round could be found. At pre-seed, absence of evidence is normal — it is
  not proof.
- **Oxailis and Bioelectrix have no independent web presence** and are confirmed only through
  their institutional registers.
- **Show HN and GitHub were low-yield for this thesis** — both are overwhelmingly developer
  tools, while the fund's pattern is consumer and small-business. Two survivors out of ~150
  Show HN posts screened. *That is a direct hit on my GitHub pipeline: a well-built instrument
  pointed at the wrong population for this particular fund.*
- Deliberately excluded: **Sandra AI** (YC F24), **EverSettled** (San Francisco HQ),
  **Whispp** (raised €5M July 2026, now covered), **Mirae Health** (Oxford, $5.4M OSE-led —
  technically seed, but already has trade press and a US deployment, so it fails the
  before-it's-obvious test).

## What I am not doing

Not crowning a new pick. The audit's sharpest charge was that my rubric moved to fit each new
winner, and declaring a fourth favourite an hour after the third was withdrawn would be the
same error with better sourcing. **Bought** and **Clearcam** are the two I would verify first
— Bought because it is the closest thing here to Creandum's own Depop, in their home region;
Clearcam because it is the only company in the project with genuinely no round at all. Neither
has been through registry, traction or competitive diligence, and both of the last two picks
died precisely there.
