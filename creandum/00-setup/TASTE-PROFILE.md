## 3. The Euro Seed 50 as a labelled dataset

Creandum's own list (euroseed50.com, Jun 2025) uses **eligibility criteria identical to
this task**: Europe HQ, raised up to and including a seed round. Full roster recovered
(n=50).

- **Geography:** UK 19, Germany 11, France 10, Sweden 4, Spain 3, NL/DK/LT/SI 1 each.
- **Vintage:** founded 2023 (16) and 2024 (18) dominate — i.e. **1–2 years old at listing**.
- **Sector:** >90% AI-native (Creandum's own stated figure); heavy vertical-agent skew.
- **Creandum converted 8 of the 50 into investments** — Maisa, Conduct, Foresight, GetVocal,
  Jack & Jill, Vesence, nexos.ai, Superscale.

**That 16% conversion rate is the decisive fact for this task.** The Euro Seed 50 is not a
publicity list; it is Creandum's own funnel. Every name on it has been seen, nominated,
debated, and either bought or passed. **Pitching one is disqualifying.** The target is a
company that *would* make next year's list and isn't on this one.

## 6. The rubric this produces (used in Phase 3)

Eight hard gates and six weighted dimensions — see `../03-shortlist/RUBRIC.md`.
The Phase-0-specific additions to the plan's original rubric are:

1. **Not on the Euro Seed 50** (hard gate, not a scoring line). 16% of that list became
   Creandum deals; the rest were actively passed on.
2. **Lead-sized room available** — a €8–20M seed they can lead, not a party round already
   syndicated by three funds.
3. **Prefers white space over adjacency.** A second robotics-brain company scores *worse*
   than a first cybersecurity or edge-AI company, all else equal.
4. **Repeat-domain operators score at a premium** (Hosted·ai precedent), not merely
   "impressive CVs".
5. **Real-world data flywheel > synthetic** (Sereact precedent).
6. **Time-to-value measured in days** (Sereact precedent), not a 9-month enterprise
   implementation.
