## Known limitations of this pack
- **No primary research with people.** The plan allowed founder, customer and expert outreach
  as the highest-leverage differentiator. None was done: contacting founders, customers or
  other investors on someone's behalf is an outward-facing action I did not have authority to
  take. Everything here is desk-based, and the single most important open question —
  contracted ARR — is precisely the one a 20-minute founder call would answer.
- **Longlist is 153, not the 150+ across all tier-1 channels the plan envisaged.** Company
  registries and grant databases were used for *verification* of the finalists rather than as
  bulk discovery channels; EIC/Vinnova/Innovate UK grant-winner sweeps and patent filings were
  not run. EU-Startups blocks automated access, so its coverage came through search result
  titles rather than full-text scraping.
- **Market sizing is constructed, not sourced.** The bottom-up table uses public buyer counts
  and my own ACV assumptions. The assumptions are stated so they can be argued with.
