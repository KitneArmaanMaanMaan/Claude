  replication located. This is the load-bearing claim of the entire thesis and it rests on
  the company's own statement plus a demo video.
- **Frogger result.** Company-published; not independently reproduced.
- **Headquarters.** YC lists London; the company's own site says Cambridge, UK; the
  registered office is a company-formation agent in Altrincham. UK-based is certain; the
  operating city is not resolved here.
- **Founding year.** YC says founded 2025; Companies House gives incorporation as 2 June
  2026. Consistent with pre-incorporation work, but the dates differ.
- **US entity.** None found, and the UK PSC is an individual rather than a corporate parent,
  so no flip is recorded. A Delaware topco is the YC default and may appear later.
- **$10M pre-seed.** Press and founder announcement only; no filing corroborates it, which is
  itself consistent with SAFEs.
