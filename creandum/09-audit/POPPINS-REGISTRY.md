# Poppins — registry diligence. The recommendation does not survive it.

Primary sources, accessed 11 September 2026. This is the check I should have run before
recommending the company, and did not.

## The entity, unambiguously

**POPPINS, SIREN 929 553 691**, SAS, incorporated **3 June 2024**, RCS Paris 5 June 2024,
38 quai Henri IV, 75004 Paris, NAF 62.01Z. Linked to Lucie Basch through **MURMURIA**
(SIREN 978 974 731), her personal holding company, which the government business API returns
as *président*, and whose *gérant* is Basch, Lucie, born 01/1992.

**Contamination avoided:** the €8M Eurazeo round and a €5M Racine2/MGEN round belong to
**BMOTION TECHNOLOGIES (POPPINS), SIREN 844 691 337** — the dyslexia neurotech company.
Tracxn's "$19M over 4 rounds" for "Poppins" is that company. Distinguished by SIREN, not name.

## Finding 1 — there have been TWO priced equity rounds, and the second was never announced

BODACC is exhaustive for this SIREN — five announcements, all retrieved.

| Event | Decided | Published | Nominal capital | Shares issued as % of post-round |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Incorporation | 2024-06-03 | A20240114963 | €10,000 | — |
| **Capital increase** | 2024-07-29 | B202401912109 | €10,000 → **€11,589** | **13.7%** |
| **Capital increase** | 2026-03-02 | B202600901663 | €11,589 → **€13,747** | **15.7%** |

The 2026 round is real cash, not an option exercise or conversion: INPI filing **PJ_56 of
26/02/2026, "Certificat du dépositaire (modification du capital social)"** — a depositary
certificate is issued only when subscription money is actually deposited in a blocked account.
It is accompanied by AG and board decisions of 02/03/2026 and updated statuts filed twice.

Immediately before it: a **notarised share-transfer act (09/02/2026)**, and Lucie Basch
personally **replaced as président by MURMURIA**. Founder rolls shares into a holdco, then a
~16% primary issuance against deposited cash, is the standard mechanical signature of a
second priced round closing.

**No press exists for it.** No company announcement, no CFNEWS entry, no trade coverage.

**Consequence:** my claim that "the cap table is angels and state-bank debt, with no
institutional priced round" and that "the seed is wide open" is **wrong**. Round 1 (angel-
priced, ~€2M equity + ~€1M debt per Les Echos) is a seed. Round 2 is unnamed but is
Series-A-shaped by dilution. **Poppins fails the eligibility gate, or sits right on its edge,
and the burden of proof is now against it.**

## Finding 2 — the revenue model was published all along, and it is not working yet

I wrote that "no revenue model is disclosed anywhere" and called it the biggest gap in the
set. That was simply a failure to read the company's own terms.

From the CGU, verbatim: *"POPPINS recevra une commission sur tous les Partages payants, égale
à vingt pour cent (20%) du montant payable au Propriétaire… avec un montant minimum de 1,50
euro."* Help centre: **20% peer, 15% professional, €1.50 floor, free lending is free.** The
€5,000 damage guarantee is bundled into that fee, not sold separately. Poppins Pro' takes 15%
with no subscription. Stripe replaced OPP as PSP on 5 August 2026.

So the model is clear. The problem is the volume underneath it:

| Metric | Value | Source quality |
|---|---|---|
| Users | **300,000 as of 3 April 2026** | Best dated third-party (Made in Marseille) |
| Users | 350,000+ | Self-reported homepage counter, **undated** |
| Objects listed | 135,000–150,000 | Self-reported |
| **Paid rentals** | **~100 per day** | Self-reported |

~100 rentals/day is ~36,500 paid rentals a year. At a €10–15 basket and a 20% take that is
roughly **€75,000–110,000 of annual net revenue**. Against 300,000 registered users, that is
about **0.03% of users transacting per day**.

This is precisely the bear case I wrote — *"a €3 rental cannot carry a take rate that funds a
company"* — except it is no longer a hypothesis. It is the observable operating position.
The first accounts (FY closed 31/12/2025) were **filed under L.232-25 confidentiality**, which
is available to small companies and is exactly what you would use if those were the numbers.

## What the registry cannot tell anyone

French registries publish only **nominal** capital. The **prime d'émission — where all the
cash sits — is never published.** So no round size or valuation is obtainable from the
register; anyone quoting one "from the registry" is inferring. The subscriber lists annexed to
the depositary certificates are filed at INPI but behind an authenticated account. And since
CJEU C-37/20 (22 Nov 2022) the **registre des bénéficiaires effectifs is no longer public**,
so no shareholder beyond MURMURIA can be identified.

To close it, three documents are obtainable from INPI/Infogreffe for a fee: acte PJ_56 of
26/02/2026, the statuts mis à jour of 02/03/2026 (which give nominal value per share, letting
you convert capital deltas into a real share count), and the greffe's état des privilèges et
nantissements for the debt picture.

## Verdict

Poppins is a good product with a real founding team and genuine consumer adoption. It is
**not** an eligible, lead-able seed for Creandum, and the monetisation evidence is weak
rather than merely unknown. The recommendation is withdrawn.
