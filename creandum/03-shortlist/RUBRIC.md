# Screening rubric

## Hard gates (binary — failing one is elimination, not a low score)

| # | Gate | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | HQ and operations in Europe | Task constraint |
| 2 | Has raised no round, pre-seed, or seed only — no Series A | Task constraint. Verified against a registry or primary announcement, never an aggregator |
| 3 | **Not on the Euro Seed 50** | Creandum converted 8 of those 50 into investments. The rest were actively passed on. Pitching one signals you read their website |
| 4 | Not a Creandum portfolio company or a direct competitor to one | Conflict |
| 5 | Founded, operating, with a real product or technical artefact | Not a concept or an agency in startup clothing |
| 6 | Round size and ownership math support a **Creandum-sized seed lead** | They lead 60–70% of seeds; Hosted·ai's was $19M |
| 7 | Verifiable from primary sources | Two-source rule |

## Scoring (100 points) — applied only to what clears the gates

| Dimension | Wt | What earns points |
|---|---|---|
| Fund-returner potential | 25 | Plausible path to €1B+. A €500M fund cannot make money on a €150M outcome |
| Founder–market fit & team density | 25 | Unfair insight and a credible right-to-win. Repeat-domain operators score at a premium (Hosted·ai precedent) |
| Why now | 15 | A specific technical, regulatory or behavioural unlock dated to *this* year |
| Evidence of pull | 15 | Customers, revenue, usage, named design partners — anything that is not a deck |
| Compounding moat | 10 | Data, network, workflow lock-in, standards position. Not "we're first" |
| Creandum fit | 10 | Stage, check size, geography, conflict-free, **white-space match**, ownership available |

### Notes on the scores that decided it

**Squid (91).** Only candidate scoring maximum on Creandum fit: European, zero conflict,
lands in a white space (grid/energy has been cold since Oct 2024), AI-native enough to
match their revealed preference, and — per the register — with **no priced round at all**,
so the entire seed is available to lead. Marked down to 20/25 on fund-returner because the
direct buyer count is genuinely small; that is the bear case and the memo argues it.

**Seeing Systems (82).** Extraordinary pull — UK Royal Marine Commandos, other UK units and
four NATO forces at seven people. Marked down hard on Creandum fit (5/10): lethal strike
drones sit outside anything in 154 portfolio companies, and many European LPAs carry
weapons exclusions, so the fund may be structurally unable to lead regardless of merit.
"European Anduril" is also now a crowded trade, which costs it the "before it's obvious"
premium. Carried as the runner-up.

**KugelAudio (77).** Genuine Lane A fit and real distribution evidence (open model,
150k+ Hugging Face downloads). Marked down on Creandum fit because of adjacency to
GetVocal (backed Nov 2025) — defensible as an infrastructure-vs-application distinction,
but a partner will raise it in the first minute.

**Lamb Labs (71).** Highest fund-returner score of the twelve — hardcoding model weights
into silicon is a real swing. Fails on practicality: a chip company needs $100M+ before
revenue, Creandum has had no active semiconductor position since IPtronics and Norstel,
and there is no evidence of pull yet (team size listed as 0, S26 batch).
